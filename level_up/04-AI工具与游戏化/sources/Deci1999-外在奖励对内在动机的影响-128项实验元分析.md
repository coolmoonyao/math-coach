# A meta-analytic review of experiments examining the effects of extrinsic rewards on intrinsic motivation

## 摘要（原文）

A meta-analysis of **128 studies** examined the effects of extrinsic rewards on intrinsic motivation. As predicted, engagement-contingent, completion-contingent, and performance-contingent rewards significantly undermined free-choice intrinsic motivation (d = **-0.40, -0.36, and -0.28**, respectively), as did all rewards, all tangible rewards, and all expected rewards. Engagement-contingent and completion-contingent rewards also significantly undermined self-reported interest (d = **-0.15, and -0.17**), as did all tangible rewards and all expected rewards.

**Positive feedback enhanced both free-choice behavior (d = 0.33) and self-reported interest (d = 0.31).**

**Tangible rewards tended to be more detrimental for children than college students**, and verbal rewards tended to be less enhancing for children than college students.

The authors review 4 previous meta-analyses of this literature and detail how this study's methods, analyses, and results differed from the previous ones.

## 关键数据速查

| 奖励类型 | 对自由选择行为（内在动机）的效应量 d | 对自陈兴趣的效应量 d |
|---|---|---|
| 参与即给奖（engagement-contingent） | **−0.40** | −0.15 |
| 完成即给奖（completion-contingent） | **−0.36** | −0.17 |
| 按表现给奖（performance-contingent） | **−0.28** | — |
| **正向反馈（positive feedback）** | **+0.33** | **+0.31** |

## 对本项目的直接含义

1. **"做题就给宠物币/登录就给能量"＝ engagement-contingent，是本元分析中损害最大的一类（d = −0.40）。** 这是"养宠物刷题"产品的默认设计，必须回避。
2. **正确做法是"正向反馈 + 按表现给奖"**：具体、指名、到步骤的肯定（d = +0.33），加上行为质量达标才给资源（performance-contingent，d = −0.28，损害最小且可通过反馈结构补偿）。
3. **儿童比大学生更容易被实物/虚拟奖励损害内在动机**——本项目面向初中生，风险等级是"高"。
4. 该文发表后有两篇同期评论（Lepper, Henderlong & Gingras, 1999；Eisenberger, Pierce & Cameron, 1999）对方法提出商榷，结论存在学术争论；但"外在奖励的条件性设计很关键"这一实践启示在各派解读中是一致的。
